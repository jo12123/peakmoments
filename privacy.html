Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how ClipForge, the video tool operated by Peak Moments ("we", "us"), handles information when a Peak Moments team member connects a YouTube account to it.

YouTube API Services

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services. By using ClipForge you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

When you connect a YouTube account and grant permission, ClipForge requests these Google permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload short videos that our team has approved to your channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel name and ID, and the details of videos ClipForge uploaded.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read views, watch time and engagement for those videos.

We do not access your Google password. Sign-in happens on Google's own pages, and Google gives ClipForge an access token limited to the permissions above.

How we use it

  • To publish approved clips to the connected channel.
  • To show performance statistics in our private dashboard and improve which clips we make.

We do not use this information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties. ClipForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we store it

ClipForge runs on a computer controlled by the Peak Moments team. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Statistics are stored in a private database on that computer. No information from your Google account is stored on this website, and this website uses no cookies or tracking.

Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while the account is connected. Disconnecting the account in ClipForge deletes its stored tokens. Video statistics stay in our private dashboard until you ask us to delete them. You can ask us to delete all of your data at any time by contacting us at the address below; we will do so within 7 days.

Revoking access

You can remove ClipForge's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account third-party connections page (also available at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).

Children

ClipForge is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
