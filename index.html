ClipForge

The in-house editing and publishing tool behind Peak Moments, a short-form channel featuring the funniest and most viral moments from creators who have given us permission to share their content.

Permission first

We only clip videos we own or that creators have explicitly allowed us to repurpose. Every source has its permission recorded.

Real editing

Each clip gets original context, commentary and captions, and credits the original creator.

Official APIs only

Clips are uploaded to our own channels through the official YouTube API Services and other official platform APIs.

What ClipForge does

ClipForge is a private application run by the Peak Moments team on its own computer. It finds highlight moments in authorized videos, edits them into vertical short videos, and, after our team approves them, publishes them to the Peak Moments YouTube channel.

When the Peak Moments team connects its YouTube account, ClipForge uses YouTube API Services to:

  • read the connected channel's name and ID, so it knows where to publish;
  • upload the approved short videos to that channel;
  • read view and engagement statistics for those videos, so we can see which clips perform well.

ClipForge is not a public service. It only accesses the YouTube channels of the Peak Moments team. See our Privacy Policy for details.
